# Lunar Site Planner v1.0.0

First archived release. A free browser tool that shows, for a lander at a given Moon site and date, when the Sun and Earth are above the real terrain skyline, how much solar power and radio contact that gives, and how to fly there.

**Live:** https://arairo.github.io/IMPROVED_lunarmoon-/

## What it does
- Beginner mission (six chapters) and a five-step Professional planner: site, date, check, what-if, report
- Sunlight, Earth visibility, solar power, battery estimate and Deep Space Network contact over the real NASA LOLA terrain (20 m around the main south-polar sites)
- All nine NASA Artemis III candidate regions with their outlines
- Flight planning from launch to touchdown (direct, phasing and low-energy routes)
- Compare sites side by side; JSON, CSV and printable reports; a read-only public API

## How accurate it is (details: docs/VALIDATION.md)
- Sun and Earth positions: within 0.007° of NASA JPL Horizons (154 comparisons, 1969–2030)
- South-pole sunlight map: correlation r = 0.92 with NASA Goddard (PGDA) lighting maps
- Shadows: 93% agreement with a real LROC photo of an Artemis III candidate region
- Sun height at five Apollo landings within 0.35° of the mission reports

A planning and education tool, not mission-certified. Built for NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 (Kuala Lumpur) by Team Dell Monitors; developed by Ammar Rasyid with AI coding assistance.
