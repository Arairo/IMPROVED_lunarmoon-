# LSIC introduction draft

**To:** [focus group lead, e.g. the Surface Power or Extreme Environments group]
**Subject:** Open browser tool for Sun/Earth/power windows at the Artemis III regions: looking for feedback

Hello [name],

We are a team from Kuala Lumpur who built Lunar Site Planner for the
NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 and have kept developing it. For any south-polar
site and date window, it shows when the Sun and Earth are above the real LOLA
skyline (20 m terrain near the Artemis III regions), the solar power,
battery need and DSN link that follow, and how to fly there.

We have checked it against independent references: JPL Horizons (0.007°),
NASA Goddard's illumination maps (r = 0.92), five Apollo landings, flown CLPS
and international missions, and a real LROC NAC mosaic of the Peak Near Cabeus
B region. Everything is open: https://arairo.github.io/IMPROVED_lunarmoon-/,
with GeoTIFF exports and a read-only API for scripts.

We would value 15 minutes of feedback from anyone in the group doing early
site or power trades: what would make it useful to you, and what we have
wrong. We are clear that it is a planning tool, not mission-certified.

Thank you,
[names, team, contact]
